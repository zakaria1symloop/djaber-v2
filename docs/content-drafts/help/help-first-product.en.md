Your agent only sells the products of your catalogue, at their price and within their stock. You need at least a name, a selling price, a stock and, ideally, a photo; the product is offered to customers once Djaber has approved it.

## Create the product

- During the guided setup: the “Product” step, then “Save and continue”.
- Afterwards, at any time: Products › “+ Add a product”.

Add one or more photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF, 5 MB max, 800 × 800 px recommended). An AI caption is generated for each photo: it is what lets the agent recognise the product when a customer sends a picture.

## Fill with AI
“Fill with AI” reads a photo of the product and fills the empty fields (name, description, category). The price is never suggested and the values you already typed are kept. Always check the fields marked “AI PREFILLED · TO CHECK”. The analysis uses credits: the number is shown on the “Analyse” button; if the analysis fails, nothing is charged.

## Djaber’s review
A new product goes through Djaber’s review before it is offered. While it is “Waiting for Djaber's review”, the AI agent does not offer it; it will once the product is approved.

- Rejected: the product is hidden from customers and from the agent. Edit it then “Resubmit”, or “Appeal” once only, within 7 days of the rejection.
- Changing the title, images, category, description or variants starts a new review. Price and stock change at once, without a new review.

## Add the rest of the catalogue

- Products › “Import”: a CSV or XLSX file.
- Connected pages › your page › “Page analysis”: Djaber reads the posts of your Facebook page and suggests products to import (free, 3 analyses per day).

Every imported product also goes through Djaber’s review.
