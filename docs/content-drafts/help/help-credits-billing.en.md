title: Credits: cost per action and packs

Every AI reply uses credits. Your plan gives you credits each period; when they run short, a credit pack takes over and never expires.

## What each action costs
Settings › Credits shows the current costs: text 1 · photo 5 · voice 3 · order 0. A text reply costs 1 credit, analysing a photo 5, transcribing a voice message 3, and creating an order costs nothing. Nothing is charged when the AI provider fails. The credits of a reply in progress are reserved until it finishes.

If Djaber changes these costs, the change is announced in advance and only applies from your next credit period.

## Follow your usage
- The meter shows the credits left in the plan, those in packs, and the renewal date.
- Above 80% of the plan credits, a warning is shown.
- “Calculator” estimates your monthly need from your conversations and the share of photos and voice messages, and tells which plan or pack covers it.
- “History” lists every debit and credit (for workspace admins), with “Export CSV”.

## When credits run out
“Credits exhausted: the AI no longer replies, manual replies still work.” New conversations land in your inbox for a manual reply, and the AI cannot be resumed on a conversation while the balance is empty. It starts again as soon as you buy a pack or the period renews.

## Buy a pack
- Click “Buy a pack” and choose a pack: its number of credits and its price are shown.
- “Pay … DA (CIB / Edahabia)” takes you to Chargily to pay with a CIB or Edahabia card, then brings you back to Djaber.
- The credits are added as soon as the payment is confirmed, and an invoice is created.

Pack credits never expire and are used after the plan credits. In the mobile app, packs are bought through Google Play or the App Store; if a store refunds a pack, the balance can go below zero and the AI stays paused until you buy new credits.

A credits promo code is typed in the PROMO CODE field of the “Change plan” window (Plan & billing), then “Use the code”: it applies at once, without changing plan. Buying packs is for the owner and the admins.
