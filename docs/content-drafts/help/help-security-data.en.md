Settings › Security holds your password, two-step verification (2FA) and the list of signed-in devices. Turn on 2FA: without your phone, a stolen password is no longer enough to get in.

## Turn on two-step verification
- Install an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Authy…).
- In Settings › Security, section “Two-step verification (TOTP)”, click “Enable”.
- Scan the QR code (or type the key), then enter the 6-digit code shown by the app.
- Djaber gives you 10 recovery codes: “Download the codes”, keep them somewhere safe, tick “I have saved my recovery codes” and finish.

From then on, Djaber asks for the app's code at every sign-in. Without your phone, choose “Use a recovery code”: each code works only once. “Regenerate codes” cancels the 10 previous codes. The 2FA code is also asked for sensitive actions, such as transferring workspace ownership or deleting your account.

## Change your password
Type the CURRENT PASSWORD and the NEW PASSWORD (at least 8 characters, with at least 1 letter and 1 digit), then “Change”. Your other devices are signed out.

## Active sessions
ACTIVE SESSIONS lists the devices signed in to your account. Sign out a device you do not know, or choose “Sign out all other devices”: they will have to sign in again with the password (and 2FA). Your current session stays open.

## Good habits
- Never share your password: invite each team member with their own account instead (Settings › Members).
- If you think someone used your account, change the password, then sign out all other devices.
