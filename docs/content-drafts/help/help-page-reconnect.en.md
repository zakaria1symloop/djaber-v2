When Meta cancels Djaber’s access to a page, the page switches to “RECONNECT” and the AI stops answering on it. Reconnecting is enough: the history of conversations and orders is kept.

## How you know

- The page shows “RECONNECT” in Connected pages, with the banner “Token revoked by Meta. The AI no longer replies on this page. History is kept.”
- You get a “PAGE” notification that opens the page directly.
- A reconnect banner also appears at the top of the app.

## Why it happens
Meta withdraws the access granted to Djaber, for example after a Facebook password change, the loss of your role on the page, or the removal of Djaber in the Facebook settings (Apps and websites).

## Reconnect

- Open Connected pages, then the page.
- Choose “Reconnect (new Meta token)”.
- In the Meta window, authorise the page again and accept every requested permission.
- The report shows “already connected · token updated” and the page’s “History” tab records “Page reconnected (new Meta token)”. The AI resumes on new messages.

## Read permission missing
The page analysis (product extraction) and “Generate from a page” need to read the page’s content. When this permission is missing, Djaber offers “Reconnect the page with the read permission”: reconnect the page and tick that permission on the Meta screen.

## If reconnecting fails
Check that you are logged in to Facebook with an account that manages the page, then try again. If the page stays “RECONNECT”, contact support from Help › Contact support: the page you came from is attached to your request.
