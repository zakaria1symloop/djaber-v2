Each person on your team has their own account and a role: Owner, Admin or Operator. The owner invites members and can also hide some sections from each of them.

## The three roles
- Owner: full rights, including members, plan and billing, workspace deletion and ownership transfer. There is one owner per workspace; nobody else can remove them.
- Admin: runs the shop: catalogue, orders, agents, pages, delivery, reports, integrations.
- Operator: handles the inbox and day-to-day orders. No deletion, no settings, and always sells at the catalogue price.

## Invite a member
- Open Settings › Members, then “Invite a member”.
- Type the e-mail, pick the role and click “Send the invitation”.
- The invitation link is valid 7 days. It shows under PENDING INVITATIONS, where you can “Resend” or “Revoke” it.

Your plan sets a number of seats, and pending invitations count as members: at the limit, a new invitation is refused. Change plan or remove a member to invite. A member who joins while the workspace is over the limit becomes READ-ONLY · PLAN.

## Manage members
- “Make admin” / “Make operator” changes the role.
- “Access by section”: untick a section to hide it from this member; it leaves their menu and is refused, even through a direct link.
- “Remove”: the member loses access to the workspace at once and their sessions are closed. Their account and past actions are kept.

## Transfer ownership
The owner can transfer the workspace to an admin: pick the NEW OWNER (ADMIN), confirm with your password (and the 2FA code when 2FA is on), then “Transfer”. You become an admin; the new owner manages billing and deletion. The transfer is irreversible without their consent.

Invitations, roles, access by section and the transfer are for the owner only. Other members can see the team list.
