Reports gathers your shop's figures by theme, each with a period, filters, totals and a CSV, XLSX or PDF export. Reports are for the owner and the admins.

## Report families
- Sales & revenue: for example “Sales by period”, “Sales by product”, “Best-selling products”.
- Orders & delivery: for example “Returns & reasons”.
- Stock: “Low stock / out of stock”, “Stock valuation”, “Dead stock”, “Inventory variances”.
- Purchases & cash: “Cash journal”, “Payments”, “Purchases by supplier”, “Margins & profitability”.
- Clients: “Top clients”, “Inactive clients”.

Periods follow Algiers time. A line sold without a cost price has no margin: it shows “—” and stays out of the average margin.

## Export
- “CSV (all rows)”: immediate for a small report.
- “XLSX (formatted + totals)”: prepared in the background.
- “PDF (current page + totals)”: A4 landscape, with your workspace logo.

Large exports run in the background: you get a notification and the file stays 7 days in Reports › Recent exports. “Copy the report link” keeps the period and the filters, to come back to or share the same view.

## Favourites and monthly e-mail
“Add to favourites” pins a report at the top of the list. “Schedule (monthly e-mail)” sends a report every month by e-mail, with the previous month's figures, on the day you choose (1 to 28, Algiers time); “Stop sending” stops it. The monthly e-mail depends on your plan: without it, manual exports stay available.

The other lists (orders, clients, products, movements, sales, purchases, cash register) also have their own “Export” button.
