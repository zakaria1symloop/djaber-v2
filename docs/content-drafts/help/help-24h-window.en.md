title: The 24 h window of Messenger and Instagram
---
Meta only allows free replies within 24 h of the customer’s last message. After that, the AI sends nothing more and what your team can send depends on the platform.

## Within 24 h
The AI agent and your team reply as usual. In the Inbox, the filter “LAST MESSAGE” › “< 24 h (reply possible)” shows the conversations you can still answer.

## After 24 h
The conversation carries the label “OUTSIDE 24 H WINDOW”.

- The AI never writes outside the window and never uses a Meta tag. A blocked AI reply shows in the thread: “AI message not sent: outside the 24 h window”.
- Messenger, with the “human agent” tag: your reply leaves with this tag and you can “Send a template message” (Order shipped, Parcel out for delivery, Order delivered, Delivery failed, Reminder scheduled), filled from the last order of the conversation.
- Messenger, without that tag: nothing can be sent until the customer writes again.
- Instagram: nothing can be sent until the customer writes again.

The conversation banner always tells you which case applies.

## Order follow-up messages
The automatic messages sent to the customer (order confirmed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered, delivery failed) only go out within the 24 h window. Outside it, the message stays in the thread, marked “Not delivered — outside the 24 h window”.

## Good practice

- Handle the “To handle” tab quickly: a conversation handed over by the agent waits for a human reply, and the window keeps running.
- To confirm an old order, call the customer from the order.
- Djaber never sends promotional messages to your customers.
