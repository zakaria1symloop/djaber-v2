Djaber answers your customers on Messenger and Instagram with an AI agent, takes their orders and brings them into the app. To get started you need a product, an agent and a connected page.

## The guided setup
When your workspace is created, a guided setup opens. Every step is optional and your progress is saved: you can leave it and come back later.

- Welcome: a summary of the steps, then “Start”.
- Stock: pick the “Simple” mode (products with their variants, orders, clients, delivery) or “Advanced” (plus cost prices and margins, suppliers, purchases, counter sales, cash register, stock counts, reports). This only changes the interface, on the web and on mobile; you can switch at any time in Settings › Stock mode.
- Product: your first item (name, price, stock, photo). See the guide “Add your first product”.
- Agent: one of the ready-made templates (Vendeur Pro, Service Client, Conseiller Produit, Réponse Express) with “Use this agent”, or “Start from scratch (guided wizard)”.
- Page: “Continue with Facebook” or “Continue with Instagram”. Your e-mail must be verified to connect a page.
- Ready: what is done and what is left, with “Test my agent”, “Import my catalogue”, “Invite my team” and “Go to home”.

## Skip or resume the setup
“Skip setup” asks you to confirm, then takes you to Home. The workspace owner can restart the guided setup from Settings › Workspace › “Resume the setup”.

## The “Get started” list on Home
On Home, the “Get started” list ticks itself from your real data:

- “Connect a page”: at least one active page;
- “Add products”: at least one active product;
- “Set up your AI agent”: at least one active agent linked to a page;
- “Make your first sale”: a first delivered order or a first sale.

## What next
Test your agent in the playground before turning it on (guide “Set up your AI agent”). Orders taken by the agent land in Orders, with a confirmation call to make.
