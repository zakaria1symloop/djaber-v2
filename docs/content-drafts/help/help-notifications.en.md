Djaber tells you about new orders, conversations to handle, parcels, credits, pages to reconnect and finished tasks. Each member chooses how they are notified, in Settings › Notifications.

## Where to see them
“Notifications”, at the bottom of the sidebar, shows a badge with the number of unread notifications. Clicking a notification opens the order, conversation or page directly.

## Choose your channels
Settings › Notifications › “Notification preferences (you)”. These settings only apply to you, not to the rest of the team.

- In-app: always on.
- E-mail and Push: chosen per event type (ORDER, PARCEL, TO HANDLE, PAGE, CREDITS, PAYMENT, STOCK, AI ISSUE…).
- Some important events are marked “always sent”; others show “not available for this channel”.
- “Sound on every new customer message”: a sound in the Inbox, on this browser only.

The message “Preferences saved” confirms every change.

## On mobile

- After the first sign-in, the app asks for permission to send notifications.
- If you refuse, Settings › Notifications shows “Notifications off — Open settings”; the app asks again only once, 7 days later.
- Tapping a notification opens the right screen, switching workspace when it concerns another of your workspaces.
- You can get push notifications on 20 devices at most. Signing out of the app stops push on that phone.
