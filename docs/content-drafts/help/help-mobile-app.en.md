The Djaber mobile app (Android and iOS) brings your workspace to your phone: inbox, orders, delivery, clients, stock, agents and analytics, with push notifications. It uses the same account as the web.

## Install and sign in

- Download the app: ⟦À COMPLÉTER : liens Google Play et App Store⟧.
- Sign in with the same e-mail and password as on the web; if two-factor authentication is on, type your code.
- If you have several workspaces, switch workspace from the app.
- After the first sign-in, the app offers fingerprint or Face ID unlock. You can turn it on or off in Account › Security.

## What is done on the web only
The app shows these items with the mention “Web only”:

- members and invitations (accepting an invitation works on mobile);
- integrations: Google Sheets, API keys, webhooks;
- editing the system messages;
- plan change, payment, invoices and subscription promo codes;
- turning two-factor authentication on or off, and recovery codes;
- danger zone: deleting the workspace, transferring ownership;
- scheduled reports and creating saved views;
- large report exports (XLSX / PDF).

## Credits on mobile
Credit packs are bought in the app, through Google Play or the App Store, at the price shown by the store. Credits are added once Djaber has verified the purchase; “Restore purchases” resends an unfinished purchase. Plan changes and subscription payments are done on the web.

## Delete my account
Account › “Delete my account”, with your password. If you own a workspace, transfer or delete it from the web first.
