Some permissions, such as Instagram messages, must be approved by Meta for the Djaber app. Until they are, the page is connected, but the AI does not answer on it yet.

## What you see

- The status “PENDING META” on the page, in Connected pages and in the connect report (“enabled automatically after approval”).
- The banner “Meta validation pending: the “Instagram messages” permission is under review. Instagram pages are visible but the AI will only reply after approval.”
- On the page: “The AI will reply as soon as the messages permission is approved.”

## Follow the approval
In the page menu, “See approval status” lists the permissions Meta is reviewing, each with its state: “APPROVED”, “IN REVIEW”, “NOT REQUESTED” or “REJECTED”.

## What you need to do
Nothing: the review concerns the Djaber app, not your page, and you have nothing to send to Meta. Activation is automatic on approval. Your Facebook pages work right now.

## Meanwhile

- Prepare your agent and link it to the page: a page pending Meta is accepted at the “Channels” step of the wizard.
- Test it in the playground, with your real catalogue, without waiting for Meta.
- Keep your other pages active: only the pending permission is affected.
