You can delete your account at any time: it is deactivated at once, then permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. During that time you can cancel and export your data.

## Delete your account
- Open Settings › Profile (or Danger zone) and click “Delete my account…”.
- Confirm with your password, and your 2FA code when 2FA is on.
- Open the link in the confirmation e-mail (valid 24 hours) to confirm the request.

As soon as you confirm, your account is deactivated: all your sessions are closed and you leave the workspaces you were a member of. After 30 days your personal data is anonymised; this cannot be undone. Invoices keep only the workspace billing details.

Without access to the app, the public “Delete my account” page (site footer) lets you make the same request with your e-mail.

## What can block the request
- You own a workspace: transfer ownership to an admin (Settings › Members) or delete the workspace (Danger zone) before deleting your account.
- The workspace you own has an unpaid invoice: settle it first.

## Cancel and export during the 30 days
Sign in with your password, or use the links of the confirmation e-mail: “Cancel deletion” reactivates the account, and “Export my data” prepares a ZIP file (JSON + CSV: profile, workspaces you were a member of, sessions and devices, terms acceptances, support requests, notifications, activity) whose download link is valid 7 days. After cancelling, your access to workspaces does not come back by itself: each workspace owner has to invite you again.

## Delete the workspace
Owner only: Settings › Danger zone › “Delete the workspace…”. Type the exact workspace name and your password, then “Schedule deletion”. Pages are disconnected and the AI stops at once; the current subscription is not refunded. Data is kept 30 days, during which every change is blocked and the owner can “Cancel deletion” (pages will then have to be reconnected). After that the workspace is permanently deleted.

To keep a copy of your shop data, use the “Export” buttons of the lists (orders, clients, products…) and of the reports before deleting.
