The creation wizard guides you in 5 steps: Identity, Knowledge, Behaviour, Channels, then Test & activation. Try your agent in the playground before turning it on: tests use no credits and never reach your customers or your orders.

## Create the agent
Open AI agents, then “New agent” (owner or admin). Pick a starting point: a template (Vendeur Pro, Service Client, Conseiller Produit, Réponse Express) or “From scratch”. The starting template can no longer be changed once the draft exists; everything else stays editable. The draft is saved as you go and can be resumed from the list (Draft status).

You can also “Generate from a page”: the AI reads the past conversations of a connected page and proposes instructions and an FAQ. It is free (3 analyses per day, 1 per page every 24 h) and nothing is created before you approve it.

## The 5 steps

- Identity: internal name (seen by your team), name shown to the customer (empty = page name), avatar, languages, tone, form of address and emojis, with a preview.
- Knowledge: catalogue, shop rules and FAQ (guide “What the agent knows”).
- Behaviour: capabilities, order taking, handoff to a human, business hours and response delay (guide “Handoff to a human”).
- Channels: the pages where the agent answers. A page has one agent only; “Move here” takes it from the other agent, after confirmation.
- Test & activation: the list of what is left to complete, the tests, then the activation.

## Test before activating

- “Test myself (playground)”: write as a customer would (Darija, Arabizi, French…), attach a photo or a voice note. Real catalogue, real delivery grid, tools in simulation: orders created are simulated (TEST-n), nothing is sent to customers, no credit is used. Limit: 60 turns per hour for the workspace.
- “Automatic test scenarios”: 8 typical conversations run on your real configuration (price in Darija, delivery in Arabizi, photo, voice note, off-catalogue, complaint, large quantity, refund). Each one is “passed” or “to review” and opens in the playground.
- “Send a test link to a colleague (expires in 24 h)”: they chat with the agent without an account, in simulation.

## Activate
Choose “Activate now”, “Activate at a date” (Algeria time) or “Save as draft”. Activation is blocked when no page is free, when your plan’s agent limit is reached (drafts do not count) or when your e-mail is not verified. The agent replies from the next message; conversations already handled by another agent do not switch.

## Edit later
The “Configuration” tab of the agent page. Every save creates a version: “Diff” and “Restore” are in “Overview”.
