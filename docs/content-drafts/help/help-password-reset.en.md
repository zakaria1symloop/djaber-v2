If you forgot your password, ask for a reset link from the sign-in page. The link arrives by e-mail and stays valid for 60 minutes.

## Get the link
- On the sign-in page, click “Forgot password?”.
- Type your account e-mail, then “Send the link”.
- The message “If an account exists for this email, a link has been sent.” is always shown, so nobody learns which addresses have an account. Check your inbox and your spam folder.

## Choose a new password
- Open the link you received: the “Reset password” page opens.
- Type the NEW PASSWORD (at least 8 characters, with at least 1 letter and 1 digit), confirm it, then “Reset”.
- You are signed in automatically, and all your other sessions are signed out.

## The link does not work
“Link expired”: the link is more than 60 minutes old or was already used. Click “Get a new link”. Only the last link requested works; a new e-mail leaves at most once a minute.

If two-step verification is on, it is still asked at the next sign-in. Without your phone, use one of your recovery codes (“Use a recovery code”).

You know your current password and only want to change it? Do it in Settings › Security.
