Settings › Plan & billing shows your plan, its usage and its renewal date. Payment is made online with a CIB or Edahabia card through Chargily, with no commitment.

## Your plan
The page shows the plan usage (pages, agents, members, products, conversations this month) and the list of plans, “Monthly” or “Yearly”. During the free trial its end date is shown; when the trial ends without a subscription, the free plan applies.

## Change plan
Only the workspace owner can change plan.
- Pick a plan (“Upgrade to …”) and the cycle, then check the details in the “Change plan” window.
- Higher plan: active at once. You pay today prorated on the days left, then the normal price at renewal, and the new plan's credits are added immediately. “Pay … DA (CIB / Edahabia)” takes you to Chargily.
- Lower plan (DOWNGRADE): applied at the end of the period, nothing to pay today (“Schedule the change”). What goes beyond the new plan's limits is PAUSED (PLAN): messages kept, AI silent, nothing deleted, and everything comes back as soon as a plan allows it.
- A PROMO CODE can lower the price; discounts do not stack.

## Renew
“Renew” is possible from 7 days before the due date. An unpaid invoice is settled with “Pay now”. Djaber reminds you 7 days before the due date, on the day and 3 days after; 7 days after the due date the free plan limits apply. Your account is never suspended for a late payment and nothing is deleted: paying the invoice restores the plan.

## Cancel
“Cancel at period end”: the plan stays active until the date shown, then the free plan applies; nothing is deleted. “Undo cancellation” keeps your plan.

In the mobile app the plan is shown read-only: plan changes and payments are made on the web.
