title: Invoices and billing details (tax ID)
---
Every subscription and every paid credit pack has its invoice, downloadable as a PDF in Settings › Plan & billing › Invoices. Fill in your billing details first so they appear on your invoices.

## Enter your company name and tax ID (NIF)
- Open Settings › Plan & billing, then “Billing info”.
- Fill in the COMPANY NAME, the TAX ID (NIF) and the ADDRESS, then save.

These details are printed on your PDF invoices. Each invoice keeps the details in place when it is issued: complete them before paying.

## Find an invoice
The Invoices list shows the number, date, label, amount and status of each invoice: upcoming, unpaid, paid, void or refunded. The payment method is shown (CIB, EDAHABIA, MANUAL, or STORE for a purchase in the mobile app).
- “Download PDF” saves the invoice.
- The invoice of the next renewal shows in advance, with its due date.

## Pay an unpaid invoice
An unpaid invoice is settled with “Pay now” (CIB or Edahabia card through Chargily). Djaber reminds you 7 days before the due date, on the day and 3 days after; 7 days after the due date the free plan limits apply, without suspending the account. An unpaid invoice also blocks the deletion of the owner's account until it is settled.

## Who can do it
Invoices and billing details are seen and edited by the workspace owner and admins. For any question about an invoice, write to support (Help › “Contact support”, “Billing” category).
