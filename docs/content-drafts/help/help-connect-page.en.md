Connect a Facebook page (Messenger) or an Instagram account so that your customers’ messages reach the Inbox and your agent can answer them. Connecting happens on Meta’s screen, in a few clicks.

## Before you start

- Your e-mail must be verified in Djaber.
- Log in to Facebook with the account that manages the page.
- Instagram: a professional account. If it is linked to a Facebook page, it is connected with that page; without a Facebook page, use “Connect Instagram”.
- The number of pages depends on your plan; the connect screen shows it (“Plan: x / y pages”).

## Connect

- Open Connected pages, then “Connect Facebook” or “Connect Instagram”.
- A Meta window opens: pick the pages to authorise and accept the requested permissions. If your browser blocks the window, allow pop-ups or choose “Open in this tab”.
- Back in Djaber, a report shows for each page: “CONNECTED”, “PENDING META”, “WARNING” (connected, automatic retry), “SKIPPED” or “LIMIT”.
- Pick the agent that will answer on these pages (“AGENT FOR THE CONNECTED PAGES”).

With Facebook, every page you authorise on the Meta screen is connected, with its linked Instagram account.

## What Djaber asks for, and what it will never do
Djaber asks to read and answer the page’s messages, and to see its posts to extract your catalogue. Djaber will never post on your page or in your name, never send promotional messages to your customers and never answer outside the 24 h window without a human action.

## Good to know

- A page belongs to one Djaber workspace only: to connect it elsewhere, disconnect it from the other workspace first.
- A page has one agent only; you can change it from the page’s “AI & agent” tab.
- “Disconnect the page” unsubscribes Djaber from the page, deletes the token and detaches the agent. Conversations and orders stay readable, and you can reconnect the page later with the same history.
