Without a connection, the mobile app stays readable but read-only. Some app updates are required to keep using it.

## Offline

- Lists and detail screens stay readable from the last loaded data, with an “Offline” banner.
- Every action button and the inbox reply box are disabled.
- Nothing is queued to be sent later: once you are back online, do the action again (reply, confirm an order, adjust a stock…).

## Updates

- Update required: a blocking screen asks you to update the app from the store; no other screen can be reached before.
- Update recommended: a banner shows on Home. “Later” hides it for 7 days.

The app checks its version each time it opens and each time it comes back to the foreground.

## Maintenance
During a Djaber maintenance, a maintenance screen shows and the app retries on its own every 60 seconds.

## Updated terms
When a new version of the terms or the privacy policy must be accepted, the app shows the documents concerned. Accept them to continue.

## Session expired
For your security, the session renews in the background. If it expires, sign in again: the app takes you back to the screen you wanted to open.
