## 2. The service

Djaber is online software that helps shops sell on Facebook Messenger and Instagram:

- an AI agent answers the shop's customers, presents the approved products and takes orders after the customer confirms the recap;
- an inbox lets the team take over a conversation at any time;
- the shop manages its orders, customers, stock, purchases, counter sales and cash register;
- parcels can be sent to the couriers ZR Express, EcoTrack or Yalidine with the shop's own credentials, and orders copied into Google Sheets;
- statistics, exports, an API and webhooks are available depending on the plan.

The available features and their limits (pages, agents, members, products, conversations per month, credits) depend on the chosen plan and are shown on the Pricing page.
