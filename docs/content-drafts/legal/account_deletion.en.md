You can delete your Djaber account at any time, from the app or without logging in. This page explains the steps, what is deleted and what is kept.

## Where to ask

- In the web app: Settings › Profile or Settings › Danger zone › "Delete my account…". You are asked for your password, and for a two-factor authentication code if it is turned on.
- Without logging in: the public "Delete my account" page (link at the bottom of every page of the site). Enter the account's e-mail address: if an account exists, a confirmation link valid for 24 hours is sent to you. To protect accounts, the answer shown is the same whether the address exists or not, and only one e-mail is sent per address every 24 hours.

## Before deleting: if you own a shop

Deletion is blocked as long as you own an active shop. Two options:

- transfer the ownership to an admin of the shop (Settings › Members);
- or delete the shop (Settings › Danger zone): the shop then enters its own 30-day period.

Deletion is also blocked while an invoice of a shop you own is unpaid.

## After the confirmation

- Your account is deactivated at once: all your sessions and devices are logged out and you are removed from the shops you are a member of.
- A 30-day grace period starts. The final deletion date is given to you by e-mail and on screen.
- During this period you can "Export my data" (JSON + CSV files of your profile, memberships and notifications; link valid for 7 days) and "Cancel deletion", from the e-mail you received or by logging in again. After a cancellation, access to the shops is not restored automatically: the owner has to invite you again.

## On the scheduled date

The account is irreversibly anonymised: your name is replaced by "Membre supprimé" (deleted member), your e-mail address, phone, picture and two-factor authentication are erased. The notes and replies you wrote in a shop stay visible under the name "Membre supprimé", and the audit log keeps track of the actions without your personal information.

## What is not affected

- The shops' data (products, orders, customers) belongs to each shop and is not deleted with a member account; it is deleted with the shop itself.
- Invoices are kept 10 years (accounting obligation), also after the shop is deleted, with the shop's billing details only.
- The data of the customers who wrote to the shops' pages follows its own rules (see "Data deletion").
