Djaber only uses the cookies and browser storage needed to run the service. Djaber sets no advertising cookie, no audience measurement tool and no social network tracker. As these items are essential, they do not require a consent banner. ⟦À VÉRIFIER : no consent for strictly necessary cookies, under Law 18-07⟧

## Cookies set

- djaber_rt — set by the Djaber server when you log in to the web app. It keeps your session open securely (unreadable by the page's scripts, sent only to the login service). Lifetime: 7 days, renewed on each use; removed when you log out.
- NEXT_LOCALE — remembers the language you chose (French, Arabic or English) to show the site in your language. Lifetime: 1 year.

## Browser storage

Some information stays in your browser without being a cookie, and is never sent to third parties:

- the workspace (shop) you opened last, to bring you back to it;
- the plan chosen on the Pricing page before signing up, erased once used;
- drafts of forms in progress (order, product, agent creation, import) and a few navigation markers (return after connecting a page, resuming the setup), erased when the tab closes or the form is saved;
- for the Djaber team only: the sound preference of the admin notifications.

## Third-party services

- Cloudflare Turnstile: when it is turned on, this anti-bot check is loaded only on some public forms (for example "Report this seller" on a tracking page) and may set its own technical items. ⟦À VÉRIFIER : cookies possibly set by Turnstile⟧
- Connecting a Facebook / Instagram page or Google Sheets: you are redirected to Meta or Google, which apply their own cookie policies on their sites.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies from your browser settings. Without the djaber_rt cookie you will have to log in again; without NEXT_LOCALE the site will be shown in the language of the address or of your browser.
