This page explains how to ask for the deletion of the data Djaber holds about you when you wrote, on Messenger or Instagram, to a shop that uses Djaber.

## Which data is concerned

When you write to a shop's page, Djaber receives for that shop: your display name and profile picture, the page-scoped Meta id, and the content of the conversation (texts, photos, voice messages, replies of the shop and of its AI agent).

## How to ask for deletion

From Facebook:

- open Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites;
- choose Djaber, then Remove, and ask for your data to be deleted.

Facebook then sends us your request. You receive a tracking code and a link to the status page of your request (/meta/data-deletion/your-code), which shows "IN PROGRESS" then "DONE" with the date. Facebook sends us an identifier specific to our app; with it we find the conversations you had with the pages connected to Djaber. If no data linked to your account is found (for example if you wrote from Instagram, or to a page that does not belong to the same Meta business), the page shows "NO DATA FOUND": then write to us as described below.

If Djaber does not appear in your apps (the most common case if you only wrote to a page), write to us at ⟦À COMPLÉTER : personal data contact e-mail⟧ with the name of the page you wrote to, your name on Facebook or Instagram and the approximate date of your messages, plus the phone number given with an order. We answer with a tracking code and the link to the status page; the deletion is done the same way as for a request coming from Facebook.

## What is deleted

In every shop that uses Djaber, the request permanently deletes:

- your conversations and all their messages;
- the photos and voice messages you sent;
- the link between your Facebook / Instagram account and the shops' customer cards;
- your customer card if it has no order;
- if you placed an order: your name (replaced by "Client supprimé", deleted customer), your phone numbers, your e-mail, your addresses and the notes on your card and on your orders.

The request is processed within 30 days at most.

## What is kept

If you placed an order, the order stays in the shop's accounting (number, items, amounts, payments, delivery wilaya and commune) but without your name, phones or address. If an order is still in progress, contact the shop before making the request: after the deletion it no longer has your details to deliver it. Information the shop already gave to a courier or copied into its own tools (for example a Google spreadsheet) is the shop's responsibility: contact it for those copies.

Without any request, messages are in any case deleted automatically 12 months after they were received.
