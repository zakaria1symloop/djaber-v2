These rules apply to everything a shop publishes or has Djaber say: products, photos, descriptions, prices, the AI agent's name and instructions, messages sent to customers. They add to the Terms of use and to Meta's rules (Facebook, Messenger, Instagram), which every shop must also follow.

## What is forbidden

- Products whose sale is forbidden or regulated by Algerian law without the required authorisations. ⟦À COMPLÉTER : list of prohibited categories adopted by the platform (Admin › Moderation › Banned words & categories)⟧
- Counterfeits, copies and imitations of brands, and listings that present them as genuine.
- Dangerous products, and unproven health claims (cure, treatment, miracle effects).
- Misleading prices, promotions, stock or delivery times.
- Inappropriate photos or photos that do not show the product sold.
- Hateful, violent, harassing, discriminatory or sexual content.
- Agent names or AI instructions that contain insults, banned words or a protected brand, or that ask the AI to deceive customers.
- Unsolicited messages (spam) and any use of the conversations other than the relationship with the customer.

## Product review

Every new product, and every change of its name, pictures, category, description or variants, is reviewed by the Djaber team before the AI agent can offer it. During the review:

- a new product stays visible in your lists marked "pending" and can be sold at the counter, but the AI does not offer it yet;
- a product already approved keeps being offered in its last approved version.

A change of price, stock or SKU does not trigger a new review.

A product that contains a word or a category banned by the platform is rejected automatically. Sensitive categories (for example electronics, health and supplements, children, cosmetics) are always reviewed by a person.

## Decisions and appeal

The Djaber team can approve the product, ask for a change or reject it, giving the reason (counterfeit, prohibited product, inappropriate image, misleading content, missing information, other) and a message. You are told in the app and by e-mail; a rejected product is no longer offered by the AI nor shown on public pages.

You can appeal a rejection once per product, within 7 days, with a message of at most 500 characters and, if needed, a supporting document (invoice, certificate) of at most 5 MB. The appeal is examined by a different team member from the one who rejected, normally within 24 hours; this decision is final.

## Shop trust level

Every shop has a trust level. A new shop has all its products reviewed. A shop with several products rejected for counterfeiting is placed under watch: all its products are then reviewed by a person, and the Djaber team may decide to suspend the shop.

## Messages sent to customers

- The AI agent only answers within the frame set by Meta: automatic replies are sent within the 24 hours after the customer's last message.
- An AI reply that contains a banned word is not sent: the conversation is handed over to your team.
- You remain responsible for your team's replies and for your agent's settings.

## Reports

Shops can report a customer, a conversation or an AI message; customers can report a seller from the tracking page of their order. Every report is examined by the Djaber team.

## Sanctions

In case of a breach, Djaber may reject or hide a product, pause an agent, block a customer across the whole platform, or suspend the shop. A suspended shop can still be viewed but can no longer change anything, and its AI agent and the reception of messages are stopped; the reason is given to it. Suspensions are decided by a person and can be lifted.
