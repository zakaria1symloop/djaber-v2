# 04 — Business rules

Enforced in the backend domain layer, never only in the UI. Each rule has a unit test (13). Baseline = V1 code; **fix** marks a closed defect.

## 1. Order workflow

### 1.1 Fields (V1 split kept)

`status` (fulfilment), `confirmationStatus` (call), `paymentStatus` (money), `deliveryStatus` (courier). CDC's single list maps: DRAFT/PENDING = AI cart in `Conversation.context.cart = {items[], recapShownAt?, orderId?}` — `recapShownAt` set when the AI sends the recap (FL-02 step 5); cart cleared on `create_order` or 7 days after `recapShownAt`; **Abandon rate** = carts with `recapShownAt` in period and no `orderId`; CONFIRMED = `confirmed`; PAID = `paymentStatus=paid`; PROCESSING = `preparing`; SHIPPED/IN_TRANSIT = `shipped` + `deliveryStatus`; DELIVERED/RETURNED/CANCELLED = same.

### 1.2 `status` transitions (V1 matrix + D14)

| From | To | Who |
|---|---|---|
| `new` | confirmed, preparing, shipped, delivered, cancelled | Operator+ (AI creates `new` only) |
| `confirmed` | preparing, shipped, delivered, cancelled | Operator+ |
| `preparing` | shipped, delivered, cancelled | Operator+ |
| `shipped` | delivered, returned | Operator+ or courier sync |
| `delivered` | returned | Admin+ (and courier sync) |
| `cancelled`, `returned` | — terminal | — |

`shipped` = parcel created at a courier **or** marked shipped manually (creates a Shipment `provider=manual`, status `sent`, optional `trackingNumber`). Manual `→ delivered` without shipment sets `deliveryStatus=delivered`. Courier events never move an order backwards. Other transitions ⇒ `422 ORDER_INVALID_TRANSITION`; terminal ⇒ `422 ORDER_TERMINAL`. Every change appends `OrderStatusHistory`. Manual create may be `new` or `confirmed` (confirm fields validated).

### 1.3 Confirmation calls (V1 guards)

Results: `picked_up → confirmed` (and `status=confirmed` if `new`); `no_answer | busy | voicemail → no_answer`; `rejected → rejected` and auto-cancel **only if** `status ∈ new/confirmed/preparing` and `deliveryStatus = not_sent` (stock restored); `picked_up` on a terminal order is logged with a warning, order unchanged. `callAttempts` = count of calls.

### 1.4 Payment

`paymentStatus`: `unpaid → partial → paid`; `pending_link` while an online link is active (P2); `refunded`. Derived from `amountPaid` (0..total; an edit of the lines / discount / fee that would bring the total below what was already paid is refused `ORDER_TOTAL_BELOW_PAID` — full-test 15/11 ROLES-02, the paid amount is never lowered silently): 0 ⇒ unpaid (paid if total = 0), ≥ total ⇒ paid, else partial. Sending `paymentStatus` without `amountPaid` is rejected (V1 let them disagree — **fix**). **COD rule (V1):** `→ delivered` while unpaid ⇒ `amountPaid = total`, `paymentStatus = paid`, income cash row = total "(COD - delivered)". Terminal orders lock payment fields.

### 1.5 Delivery (`deliveryStatus`)

`Order.deliveryStatus`: `not_sent → pending (queued) → sent → in_transit → out_for_delivery → delivered | failed | returned`. `Shipment.status` (courier level, `ShipmentStatus`) is `pending | sent | picked_up | in_transit | out_for_delivery | delivered | failed | returned | cancelled | stale`; `picked_up` is a status (pill RAMASSÉE) and maps to `deliveryStatus=in_transit`; `cancelled` and `stale` leave `deliveryStatus` unchanged. Raw courier statuses are mapped per provider in one table (05-integrations §2); unknown raw status ⇒ event stored, status unchanged. `failed` keeps `status`, sets `Shipment.failureReason ∈ FailureReason (address | unreachable | refused | damaged | other)`; actions: re-attempt (adapter) or mark returned. Terminal courier `delivered` ⇒ order `delivered` (COD rule); `returned` ⇒ order `returned` after the merchant confirms "return received" (stock restored then).

### 1.6 Required data

Create: ≥ 1 item, client name, phone (validated). Confirm: + wilaya, commune, address (or stop-desk), delivery fee computed. Ship via courier: + courier account. Missing ⇒ dialog "address missing" state (FIG).

## 4. Money

- DZD, integer dinars. Catalogue `Decimal(10,2)` from V1 is rounded at migration.
- `lineTotal = unitPrice × qty − lineDiscount`; `subtotal = Σ lineTotal`.
- `deliveryFee = 0 if home delivery && threshold && (subtotal − discount) ≥ threshold else grid[wilaya][home|stopdesk]` (customer price, D15; `threshold = DeliveryFeeRule.freeAbove ?? Workspace.freeDeliveryThreshold`); Admin+ may override per order. Free delivery is a **home-delivery** rule (prisma `freeAbove` « free home delivery »): a stop-desk order always pays the stop-desk price — said on the fee grid bar, the order form fee line (« Offerte dès … à domicile seulement ») and in the AI `quote_delivery` result (`freeFor: 'home'`, `note`) and prompt (journey 16/11 J08-3). The threshold is read on the subtotal **after the discount** on every path (create, items edit, address change, the order page's fee explanation, the order form, the address dialog) — J08-2. A negative or non-integer fee / threshold is refused (`validation.price`), never turned into another amount (J08-1).
- `total = subtotal − discount + tax + deliveryFee` (V1 order), `sale.total = subtotal − discount + tax`, `purchase.total = Σ(unitCost×qty) + tax + shipping`.
- Prices snapshotted on lines.
- **Named figures (used verbatim in every KPI label):** **Revenue** = Σ `Sale.total` (soldAt) + Σ `Order.total` (delivered, deliveredAt) (V1 reports, D16); **Net revenue** = Revenue − delivery fees; **Pipeline** = Σ `Order.total` status ∈ new/confirmed/preparing/shipped; **Cash collected** = Σ `Order.amountPaid` (orders placed in the period) + Σ `Sale.amountPaid` (completed counter sales, soldAt) — the same money as Caisse and the « Paiements » report (UX-052, 10/11); **AI revenue** = Revenue restricted to AI orders (analytics › agents/channels, labelled). The line under a Revenue amount (Home tile) counts the rows of that amount, same period and same definitions: delivered orders (deliveredAt) and completed counter sales (soldAt) — "1 commande livrée · 1 vente"; never the orders created in the period (UX-063). **One name, one figure** (journeys 16/11 J11-2): the Home tile is « Revenus (30 j) » (the Revenue, never « Chiffre d'affaires »); **Panier moyen** = Revenue ÷ (completed counter sales + delivered orders) of the period (Ventes par période); the counter-sales list shows « Panier moyen comptoir » = Σ `Sale.total` ÷ count; **Ventes brutes** = line amounts before document discounts and without delivery fees (Ventes par produit / par catégorie, Produits les plus vendus, Statistiques › Top produits / Produits — never labelled « Revenus », ANALYTICS-03); the product page « Ventes 30 j » (`pricing.sales30d`) and the units sold of its margin (EXP-02: the fixed expenses' share, the list margin and its `minMargin` / `maxMargin` filters) are the same figure for one product: delivered orders (deliveredAt) + completed counter sales (soldAt) over the reports' « 30 j » period (today and the 29 days before, Africa/Algiers) — an order not delivered yet is « Réservé » stock, never sold (journey 16/11 J14-10); **Marge moyenne** = (Ventes brutes − COGS) ÷ Ventes brutes over the lines with a known cost price, the same number in Ventes par produit and Statistiques › Produits; a figure restricted to a population gets a qualified name (Meilleurs clients: « Revenus des clients identifiés », walk-in sales have no client; its count column « Commandes livrées », its date « Dernier achat » = last order or sale; Produits les plus vendus: « Commandes et ventes »). The « Paiements » report chart splits the money collected (rows « in ») by mode; payments to suppliers are not added to it (J11-1). The Caisse tiles are the journal's « Entrées » / « Sorties » (every row of the selection: the receipt of a counter sale cancelled later and its refund both appear), never labelled « encaissé » — « Encaissé » is only **Cash collected** (journey J10-3). Mode « COD » = cash collected at delivery (the row the delivered status writes); a cash deposit recorded before delivery is « Espèces » on the order, in Caisse and in the reports (journey J05-6).
- P&L: COGS = Σ lines × cost price snapshot (variant cost when variant); a line sold **without a cost price** has no margin: « — » in Ventes par produit / par catégorie (cost and margin `null`), out of the average margin, and the P&L adds the line `unknownCostSales` (« Ventes sans coût d’achat ») when > 0 (journeys 16/11 J06-4); the same rule on every screen (J11-3): Statistiques › Produits « Marge moyenne » leaves those lines out and names their amount (« hors 900 DA vendus sans prix de revient »); a stock line without a cost price has cost and value at cost `null` (« — ») in Valorisation du stock / Stock dormant / Écarts d'inventaire, stays out of every « valeur au coût » total (report KPI `unknownCostUnits` « Unités sans prix de revient »; `/stock/overview`, `/products/stats` and Home `stockValue` carry `unknownCostUnits` and the tiles say « hors N articles sans prix de revient »), and the potential margin compares retail and cost of the lines with a known cost only; a cost is unknown until it is typed or the first receipt gives it (04 §20, J07-2); expenses = manual cash expenses + product expenses + return costs; **purchases are not subtracted again** (**fix**).
