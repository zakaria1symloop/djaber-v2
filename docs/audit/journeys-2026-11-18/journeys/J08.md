# Journey J08 (re-run) — Delivery — tested 18/11/2026 14:25–14:58 (Africa/Algiers)

Live https://djaber.vercel.app, fresh own shop « Boutique de QA J08r » (owner `qa-j08r-owner@test.djaber.local`, signed up
through the real sign-up page at 14:25, onboarding stock mode « Avancé », preset agent « Vendeur Pro », page step « Plus tard »).
No courier account. Scripts: `.local/qa/journeys/J08r/scripts/` (logs `.local/qa/journeys/J08r/*.log`), screenshots
`.local/qa/journeys/J08r/shots/`, CSV `.local/qa/journeys/J08r/exports/livraisons.csv`.

Summary: 7 bugs. 3 Major (J08r-1, J08r-2, J08r-3), 3 Minor, 1 Cosmetic (a group of 7 small texts).
- 16/11 retest (12 IDs): 10 fixed, 2 still broken (J08-1 partly, J08-12 only item 10).
- 15/11 retest (18 IDs): 13 fixed, 2 still broken (DELIVERY-01 and DELIVERY-12 `job=`, both partly), 3 cannot test (no courier).

The free-delivery rule is now the same on every screen: grid bar, order form, order page, quote API and the AI `quote_delivery`.
It applies at home only, on the subtotal after the discount. The COD and first-attempt figures now equal the Caisse.
Self-delivery parcels without a tracking number get the right actions and are counted in « échecs du jour ».

The problems left are in money inputs:
- the fee grid and the threshold still turn « 1e3 » into 13 and drop decimals without a word;
- the order form's fee override turns « 300 » into 3 000 after the field is cleared;
- the return of a self-delivered parcel takes a 300 DA « Espèces » expense out of the Caisse by default.

Note: the API was unreachable for about one minute at ~14:35 (CORS error + WebSocket 502). The order form showed « Pas de
connexion » and kept its draft, which is the expected offline state, so no bug is recorded for it. The order was saved again afterwards.

## Bugs

### J08r-1 — Fee grid and « Livraison offerte » still save « 1e3 » as 13 DA and drop decimals without a word; a minus sign blanks the cell, or switches free delivery off (DELIVERY-01 / J08-1 leftover)
- Page: /fr/delivery/fees (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps:
  1. Grille des frais, row 31 Oran, DOMICILE: type each of `1.5`, `-100`, `-`, `1e3`, `2,5`, `12.000`, `1000000`, `1000001`, followed by Tab.
  2. « Livraison offerte à partir de » (switch on): type `-5`, `1e3`, `1.5`, `12500,50`, `0`, `10000`, each followed by Enter.
  3. With the threshold at 10 000 DA, type `-5` and press Enter.
- Expected: integer DZD. A negative or non-integer amount is refused with an inline message and never turned into another
  amount (04 §4: « A negative or non-integer fee / threshold is refused (`validation.price`), never turned into another amount (J08-1) »).
- Actual (s05, s05b, probe-minus):
  - `1e3` → `PUT /delivery/fees/31 {"homePrice":13}` → 200. The same in the threshold: `PATCH /workspace {"freeDeliveryThreshold":13}` → 200.
  - `1.5` → 1, `2,5` → 2 (PUT 200), and the threshold `12500,50` → 12 500 (PATCH 200). No message.
  - `-100` in a cell: the field keeps only « - ». After Tab it is blank, nothing is sent and no message appears. The saved 700 comes back only after a reload.
  - Threshold at 10 000, then `-5` + Enter → `PATCH /workspace {"freeDeliveryThreshold":null}` → 200. The switch turns off, so the shop loses free delivery without a word.
  - Threshold `0` + Enter: the field goes back to 12 500 with no message.
  - Fixed since 16/11: `1000001` → inline « Montant entier de 0 à 1 000 000 DA. »; Arabic digits `٥٠٠` → 500; `2500,00` → 2 500.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J08r/s05.log · s05b.log · probe-minus.log · shots fr-s05b-row31.png · fr-s05b-free-minus.png · fr-probe-minus-threshold.png
- Likely cause: packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx / number-parse.ts still keep the digits of `1e3` and truncate decimals instead of refusing them. The threshold bar maps an unparsable draft to `null` (off).
