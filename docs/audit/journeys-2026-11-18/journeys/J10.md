### J10r-1 — Paiements report: « TOTAL (2 lignes) 6 400 » adds money received and money paid out
- Page: /fr/reports/payments?period=today (admin, « Boutique Test Équipe », fr, 1440 px)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Admin: order #CMD-1030 paid 3 400 DA cash; purchase #ACH-1014 paid 3 000 DA at reception (today's only payment rows). 2. Rapports › Paiements › Aujourd'hui. 3. Read the tiles and the TOTAL row.
- Expected: the total row agrees with the tiles: 3 400 received, 3 000 paid out, net 400 (or one total per direction).
- Actual: tiles « 3 400 DA ENCAISSÉ · 3 000 DA PAYÉ · 2 PAIEMENTS », rows « CMD-1030 · Encaissement · 3 400 » and « ACH-1014 · Décaissement · 3 000 », then « TOTAL (2 lignes) 6 400 ». The MONTANT column is always positive (the direction is a separate column) and the total sums it in both directions. Cause: `apps/api/src/modules/gds/reports/definitions.ts:341` `amount: e.amount` for both directions; the total row sums `amount`.
