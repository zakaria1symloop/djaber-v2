## Bugs

### J06r-1 — « Modifier le paiement » changes the mode on the sale only: the payment, the Caisse row, the reports, the ticket and the theoretical cash keep the old mode
- Page: /fr/sales/[id] › Actions › Modifier le paiement (#VNT-1008 Carte, #VNT-1002 CCP) (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Sale #VNT-1002 « 12 500 DA CCP » (validated, paid). 2. Actions › Modifier le paiement › MODE « Espèces » › Enregistrer. 3. Read the sale page, Caisse, Paiements report, the ticket PDF, the close dialog. 4. Open « Modifier le paiement » again.
- Expected: the dialog says « #VNT-… · mode et note »: the mode picked is the mode of the money taken. The payment row, the cash row and the reports follow it, so a seller who clicked « Carte » for a customer who paid cash can fix it before counting the drawer (theoretical cash + 1 800 / + 12 500). On a closed day the change is refused (`CASH_DAY_CLOSED`) like the other writes.
- Actual: PATCH `/sales/:id {"paymentMethod":"cash","notes":null}` → 200. The dialog now opens on « Espèces » (sale `paymentMethod: cash`). Everything else still says the old mode: sale page PAIEMENTS « 12 500 DA CCP », Caisse row « Vente #VNT-1008 · VENTE · Carte +1 800 », Paiements report « VNT-1008 … Carte », ticket PDF « Paiement : Carte ». API payments stay `1800/card`, and `/cash/stats.day.theoreticalCash` stays 12 950. The merchant sees no change on any screen, and the drawer count stays off by the amount. It was also accepted on the closed 18 nov. Reproduced on 2 sales (#VNT-1008 card → cash on the closed day, #VNT-1002 ccp → cash).
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J06r/probe-closed.log (« (c) API VNT-1008 cash ["1800/card"] », rows « income sale card 1800 ») · probe-mode.log · probe-mode3.log (« before … CCP », « after … CCP », « reopened dialog mode cash », « theoretical cash 12950 ») · exports/ticket-VNT-1008-after-mode-change.pdf · shots/fr-probe-mode3-after.png
- Likely cause: apps/api/src/modules/gds/sale.service.ts:289 updates `sale.paymentMethod` only. The `SalePayment` rows and their cash rows are not rewritten through `SaleEffects`, and the closed-day check is not run.

### J06r-2 — A minus typed in an amount field freezes it: the caret jumps in front of « - » and every digit is refused. In the closing dialog the « - » counts as 0, and « -1 000 » counts as +1 000
- Page: /fr/cash › Clôturer la journée (COMPTÉ EN CAISSE), /fr/sales/new (MONTANT REÇU, REMISE), /fr/cash › + Mouvement manuel (MONTANT) (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Theoretical cash negative (manual row −30 000, deleted afterwards): close dialog « Solde théorique −17 050 DA ». 2. In COMPTÉ EN CAISSE type `-1000` key by key. 3. Empty it, type `1000`, press Home, type `-`. 4. Same keys in the sale sheet and the manual-row dialog.
- Expected: one reading of what is typed. A field that refuses negatives refuses the « - » with its message. A field that keeps it keeps the digits after it. The gap is computed from the number shown.
- Actual: after `-` the field reads « - » with the caret at 0 (before the minus), so `1`, `0`, `0`, `0` are each refused (red flash). The field stays « - » and the gap reads « Écart +17 050 DA · Crée une ligne CORRECTION +17 050 DA » (counted = 0). With the minus put in front of 1 000 the field shows « -1 000 », but the gap is « +18 050 DA » (counted read as +1 000; −1 000 would give +16 050). `fill('-17050')` shows « -17 050 » with gap « +34 100 DA ». The submit would send `digitsToInt(counted) ?? 0`, without the sign. The same freeze happens in MONTANT REÇU, REMISE and the manual MONTANT (`-500` typed → « - »). Reproduced in 2 scripts.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J06r/probe-neg.log (« key 1 -> "-" », « 1000 then minus at start -> "-1 000" … Écart / +18 050 DA », « fill -17050 … +34 100 DA ») · probe-minus.log (« -=>"-"@0  5=>"-"@0 ») · s18.log (« counted typed -1000 -> - ») · shots/fr-probe-neg-counted.png
- Likely cause: packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx `NumberInput`: the caret is put back after `r.caretDigits` digits (0 after a lone « - »), i.e. in front of the minus, so the next key gives « 5- », which `parseIntegerInput` refuses. apps/web/src/features/gds/cash-dialogs.tsx `closingGap` / `digitsToInt` drop the sign that the field now keeps. This affects every `MoneyInput` (J05r-1 family).
