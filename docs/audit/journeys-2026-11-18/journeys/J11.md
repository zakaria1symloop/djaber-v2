## Bugs

### J11r-1 — Paiements report: the table TOTAL « 65 600 DA » adds money in and money out
- Page: /fr/reports/payments?period=today (owner, fr / ar / en, 1440). Same in the CSV and the XLSX (rows only, see J11r-2).
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug (figures)
- Steps: 1. Day with 47 600 DA collected (orders + counter sales) and 18 000 DA paid to the supplier (#ACH-1001 8 000 espèces, #ACH-1002 10 000 virement). 2. Rapports › Paiements, Aujourd'hui.
- Expected: the KPIs say « 47 600 DA ENCAISSÉ · 18 000 DA PAYÉ ». The footer of a table with a « Sens » column gives either both totals (« Encaissements 47 600 · Décaissements 18 000 ») or the net (29 600). It should not give their sum. J11-1 removed the mix from the chart (04 §4: « payments to suppliers are not added to it »), and the footer should follow the same rule.
- Actual: « TOTAL (15 lignes) … 65 600 » (ar « المجموع (15 سطرًا) 65 600 », en « TOTAL (15 rows) 65 600 »). 65 600 = 47 600 in + 18 000 out. No other screen shows this number.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J11r/ui-rep1-today.txt (/reports/payments) · ui-ar.txt · ui-en.txt · shots/fr-rep1-today-reports_payments_period_today.png · API GET /reports/payments?period=today → `kpis received 47600 / paid 18000`, `totals.amount 65600`
- Likely cause: the `amount` column of the payments definition is `sum: true` over every row, whatever its `direction`.
