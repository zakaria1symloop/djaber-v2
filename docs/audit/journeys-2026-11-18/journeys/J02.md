Live https://djaber.vercel.app. Own fresh shop « Boutique de QA J02r » (owner `qa-j02r-owner@test.djaber.local`), signed up through the real sign-up page. Stock mode « Avancé » was chosen in the tunnel, then « Passer la configuration ». The shop is on the Pro trial (14 days, unlimited products).
Every browser session ran through `tools/qa/pw-kit.mjs`, one browser at a time, closed after each script. The allow list held only `PATCH /workspace/onboarding` and `PATCH /workspace`, for this account only.
Scripts and logs: `.local/qa/journeys/J02r/scripts/`, `.local/qa/journeys/J02r/s*.log`. Screenshots: `.local/qa/journeys/J02r/shots/`. Files (import CSV, error report, export XLSX, labels PDF): `.local/qa/journeys/J02r/files/`.

### J02r-1 — Product with variants: the full form still edits a base price, old price and cost that show nowhere, and the bulk-price preview quotes that hidden base price (« T-shirt coton bio 2 000 → 2 200 »)
- Page: /fr/products/[id]/edit, /fr/products?sheet=<id>, /fr/products › bulk « Prix » (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps:
  1. « T-shirt coton bio » has variants S 1 500 / M 1 600 / L 1 700 / XL 1 900.
  2. Quick-edit sheet: « PRIX DE VENTE » is now locked with « Prix fixé par variante : modifiez-le dans la fiche du produit. » (the J02-3 fix). « ANCIEN PRIX » is still editable: type 2 500 › Tab › « ● Ancien prix modifié · enregistré ».
  3. Follow the hint to the full form › « Modifier ». The first field of the « PRIX » block is « PRIX DE VENTE (DA) * » = 1 500. Type 2 000 › Enregistrer (`PATCH {price:2000}` → 200, the product goes back to validation).
  4. Products › tick the T-shirt › « Prix » › Augmenter de 10 %.
- Expected: a product with variants is priced by its variants only (PRODUCTS-03, product-card.ts « never the base price »). The form either locks the base price, old price and cost, or says that they are not used. The bulk preview shows the variants' prices (1 500 – 1 900 → 1 650 – 2 090).
- Actual:
  - The form takes the base price 2 000, and the sheet takes the old price 2 500. The table, the grid, the page, the card, the pickers and the export never show either value, so the merchant who follows the sheet's hint edits a dead field and sees nothing change.
  - The bulk dialog then previews « Aperçu : T-shirt coton bio 2 000 → 2 200 ». The variants actually move from 1 500 / 1 600 / 1 700 / 1 900 to 1 650 / 1 760 / 1 870 / 2 090, and the hidden base price also moves to 2 200.
  - The export writes no old price for the variant rows.
- Likely cause:
  - `apps/web/src/features/catalog/product-form/` keeps the price / old price / cost fields editable when `hasVariants`.
  - The quick-edit sheet locks only the price.
  - `features/catalog/products-list/bulk.tsx:250` previews `computeBulkPrice(r.price, input)` instead of the variants' range.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J02r/s10.log (`api tshirt 2000 2500 … priceRange {min:1500,max:1900}`) · shots/fr-s10-edit-form.png · fr-s10-sheet-old.png · s20r.log (« Aperçu : T-shirt coton bio 2 000 → 2 200 », `tshirt after 2200 [["S",1650],…,["XL",2090]]`) · shots/fr-s20r-bulk-price.png
