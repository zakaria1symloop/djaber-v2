# Journey J14 — Phone, RTL, keyboard (J05 orders + J06 counter sales / cash at 390 px in Arabic, keyboard only) — tested 16/11/2026 20:30–21:45 (Africa/Algiers)

Live https://djaber.vercel.app, fresh own shop « Boutique de QA J14 » (owner `qa-j14-owner@test.djaber.local`, signed up
through the real sign-up page, onboarding with stock mode « Avancé »). Scripts: `.local/qa/journeys/J14/scripts/` (one per
step), logs `.local/qa/journeys/J14/*.log`, screenshots `.local/qa/journeys/J14/shots/`.

Summary: 10 bugs — 2 Major (J14-1, J14-2), 7 Minor, 1 Cosmetic. The shell is now keyboard- and phone-friendly (drawer,
dialogs, focus return, shortcuts, phone search, focus rings all fixed); what breaks is inside the two daily forms: the order
form's lines are unreadable on a phone (J14-1) and the counter-sale sheet cannot be finished with the keyboard (J14-2).
Every money / stock figure matched across the screens at 390 px in Arabic, except the product page's « مبيعات 30 يومًا »
(J14-10) and the known J05-6 payment-method label. Retest of 27 IDs of 15/11: 20 fixed, 2 still broken (MOBILE-A11Y-07 and
SALES-CASH-23, both partly), 5 cannot test.

## Bugs

### J14-1 — New order form on a phone: the product name of every line is squeezed to 4 px (SALES-CASH-03 fixed only in the sale sheet)
- Page: /ar/orders/new and /fr/orders/new (owner, ar + fr, 390 × 844)
- Severity: Major
- Type: UX
- Steps: 1. Phone width, Commandes › + Nouvelle commande. 2. Add « QA J14 Savon olive » and « QA J14 Robe kabyle brodée… ».
- Expected: each line shows which product it is (the same fix as the « Nouvelle vente » sheet, where the name now has its own row).
- Actual: the name cell measures 4 × 21 px for both lines (scrollWidth 115 / 380 px): only « ( » or one letter is visible; the SKU and « (sans variante, stock 36) » wrap one word per line in a 30 px column next to the price box. With several lines the seller cannot tell them apart before saving. Same in French and Arabic. In Arabic the sticky footer's « تم حفظ المسودة على هذا الجهاز » also wraps one word per line.
- Likely cause: packages/ui/src/order-form.tsx `OrderLineRow`: `flex flex-wrap … sm:flex-nowrap` with the name in `min-w-0 flex-1` (basis 0) next to `w-28` price + stepper + `w-24` total — on the first flex line the name gets 0 width instead of wrapping onto its own row.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J14/shots/fr-s10-order-lines.png · ar-s09c-items.png · s10.log (« QA J14 Savon olive 4x21 sw=115 »)

### J14-2 — « Nouvelle vente » sheet with the keyboard: Tab never gets past the product search, so a sale cannot be finished without a mouse
- Page: /ar/sales/new (owner, ar, 390 × 844; same component at every width)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Ventes comptoir, Tab to « + Nouvelle vente », Enter (focus on ✕ ✓). 2. Tab (product search), type « مسك », Tab, Enter: the line is added. 3. Press Tab repeatedly / Shift+Tab / Esc.
- Expected: Tab goes on to the line (quantity, price, ✕), discount, client, payment, amount received and « تأكيد البيع » (07 « WCAG 2.1 AA … keyboard », 2.1.1).
- Actual: after Enter on a result the focus lands on the dialog container. Tab then cycles ✕ → search → the 5 product results (the list opens whenever the search has focus) → dialog container → ✕ … forever (14 presses recorded); Shift+Tab from the search stays on the dialog container (4 presses); Esc in the search opens « المغادرة دون حفظ؟ » instead of closing the result list. The lines, discount, client, payment method, amount received and the validate button are unreachable from the top of the sheet. (When the script put the focus on « المبلغ المستلم », Tab reached « تأكيد البيع » in 2 presses and the sale was saved, #VNT-1004.)
- Likely cause: apps/web/src/features/gds/product-picker.tsx — the result `<ul>` is rendered while the search is focused and unmounted on blur; leaving the last result unmounts the list under the focus, so Radix FocusScope sends the focus back to the dialog container; its Escape handler does not stop the dialog's own Escape.
- Evidence: s08b-ar-390.log (« 14 tabs after the pick », « Shift+Tab x4 ») · s08d-ar.log (Esc → leave guard) · shots ar-s08-390-sheet-lines.png

### J14-3 — Keyboard focus is dropped to the page body after picking a product in the order form and after saving a dialog
- Page: /ar/orders/new, /ar/orders/[id] « تسجيل دفعة » (owner, ar, 390 px)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. /orders/new, Tab to « أضف منتجًا », type « Savon ». 2. Press ↓ (nothing), Tab to the result, Enter. 3. Press Tab. 4. On an order, « تسجيل دفعة » › Enregistrer with Enter.
- Expected: after the pick the focus stays in the search (to add the next product) or moves to the new line; after a saved dialog it returns to the opener or the next control (MOBILE-A11Y-03 rule).

Same `<body>` after « حفظ » of the payment dialog, after « تأكيد البيع » and after « حفظ الطلب » (new order page). Esc / cancel do return the focus correctly (see retest MOBILE-A11Y-03).
- Evidence: s09c-ar.log (« Enter on result BODY », « Enter (variant product) BODY ») · s11-pay.log (« focus after payment saved BODY ») · s08e.log

### J14-4 — Phone: the sticky save bar of the order form hides the focused field

- Type: UX

- Expected: the focused control is visible (WCAG 2.2 2.4.11; the bar is 82 px in Arabic, 160 px in French where its three buttons stack).
