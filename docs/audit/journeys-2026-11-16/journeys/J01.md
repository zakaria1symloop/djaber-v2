# Journey J01 — New merchant, day 1 — tested 16/11/2026 17:00–17:45 (Africa/Algiers)

Live https://djaber.vercel.app. One brand-new shop, created through the real pages: landing → Tarifs → Annuel → Pro « Essai gratuit 14 j » → sign-up (`qa-j01-owner@test.djaber.local`). Then the 6 tunnel steps, Home, the plan offer, a skip + « Reprendre la configuration » + a second walk through the tunnel, sign-out / sign-in, and the key screens in ar at 390 px and in en at 1440 px.
Every browser session ran through `tools/qa/pw-kit.mjs`. The allow list held only `PATCH /workspace/onboarding` and `PATCH /workspace`, for this account only. The plan intent was carried from the sign-up browser to the next scripts by copying that browser's own localStorage (`djaber.planIntent`) before the real login, so the scripts act as « the same browser ».
Scripts and logs: `.local/qa/journeys/J01/scripts/`, `.local/qa/journeys/J01/s*.log`. Screenshots: `.local/qa/journeys/J01/shots/`.

Summary: 0 Blocker, 3 Major, 4 Minor, 5 Cosmetic. The tunnel itself now works end to end in fr / ar / en and at 390 px. Resume, F5, the skip confirmation, « Reprendre la configuration », the plan offer and the pending-moderation messages all behave as written. The serious problems sit next to the tunnel: the shared number field, live pack data, and the preset agent's empty wizard.

## Bugs

### J01-1 — Typed price / stock: the caret jumps in front of the decimal mark, so « 12,5 » becomes 125 and « 2 500,00 » becomes 250 000 DA
- Page: /fr/onboarding/first-product (step 3), and /fr/products/new « Prix de vente ». It is the shared `NumberInput` / `MoneyInput` (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Click the Prix field. 2. Type `1`, `2`, `,`, `5` one key at a time. 3. Leave the field. 4. Do the same with `2 500,00` on /products/new, and with `3.7` in Stock.
- Expected: « 2 500,00 » is read as 2 500, and « 12,5 » as 12 with « ,5 » shown and dropped on blur. This is what the code comment of the 15/11 fix promises (`packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx` NumberInput, « ORDERS-01, PRODUCTS-01… : « 2 500,00 » is 2500 »). Alternatively, a refusal or a hint (ONBOARDING-07 expected).
- Actual: after the `,` the field shows « 12, », but the caret is put back after the 2nd digit, which is before the comma. The next `5` lands in the integer part: « 125, », then **125** on blur. The same happens with « 2 500,00 » → « 250 000, » → **250 000 DA**, « 1.5 » → 15, and « 3.7 » in Stock → « 37. » → **37**. No message is shown, and the form submits the wrong amount. Reproduced 4 times (step 3 twice, « Ajouter un autre produit », /products/new).
- Likely cause: `packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx` NumberInput. The `useLayoutEffect` puts the caret back after `caretDigits` digits of `shown`, and the fraction (« , ») is not a digit, so the caret lands in front of it. This means the 15/11 fixes ORDERS-01, PRODUCTS-01, DELIVERY-01, STOCK-02, SETTINGS-09 and SALES-CASH-07 only work for pasted values, not for typed ones.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J01/shots/fr-s3-product-wrongchars.png · fr-s11-products-new-price.png · s3.log / s11.log (« typed "2 500,00" -> "250 000," -> blur "250 000" »)
