# Journey J02 — Catalogue build — tested 16/11/2026 17:26–18:00 (Africa/Algiers)

Live https://djaber.vercel.app. Own fresh shop « Boutique de QA J02 » (owner `qa-j02-owner@test.djaber.local`), signed up through the real page. Stock mode « Avancé » was chosen in the tunnel, then « Passer la configuration ». The shop is on the Pro trial (14 days, unlimited products).
Every browser session ran through `tools/qa/pw-kit.mjs`. The allow list held only `PATCH /workspace/onboarding` and `PATCH /workspace`, for this account only.
Scripts and logs: `.local/qa/journeys/J02/scripts/`, `.local/qa/journeys/J02/s*.log`. Screenshots: `.local/qa/journeys/J02/shots/`. Files (images, import CSV, error report, export XLSX): `.local/qa/journeys/J02/files/`.

Catalogue built: 19 products.
- 9 made in the form: simple with photo and promo old price; 4 variants with their own prices; 125 000 DA; 2 Arabic names; « Accepter les commandes en rupture » at stock 0; 2 services; one with a unit.
- 1 made with « Générer des variantes » (3 × 2 = 6 variants, 3 of them repriced).
- 6 from an import file of 13 rows (6 valid, 7 refused).
- 1 duplicate.
- 2 with a banned word (refused automatically).

Categories: 7 (2 levels, Arabic), then 1 deleted with reassignment. Units: 1 custom.

Sold on the counter: 1 sale. Ordered: 1 order (« Nouvelle », not confirmed). Stock adjustments: +10 / −3 / reversal / +2 on a variant.

Summary: 0 Blocker, 2 Major, 5 Minor, 4 Cosmetic.
- Prices match across the table, the product page, the quick-edit sheet, the export, the order and sale lines, and the saved order and sale. A price change shows everywhere at once. Old orders and sales keep their price.
- Stock always equals the sum of the movements, on 6 screens.
- Moderation hides every pending or refused product from the AI.
- What breaks is the Messenger card of a product with many variants, the import's price limit, and the places that still read the unsold base price or count low stock differently.

## Bugs

### J02-1 — The Messenger card of a product with many variants cuts off its price (« 40 · Noir · … · 42 · Blanc · 4 50… »)
- Page: /fr/products/[id] › « Aperçu carte Messenger » (owner, fr, 1440). It is the very card the AI sends to customers (`productCards` + `cardElement`, the same function as the send).
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Create « Chaussures sport Atlas » with « Générer des variantes »: `Taille : 40, 41, 42` × `Couleur : Noir, Blanc`, priced 4 500 DA, with the 3 « Blanc » at 4 800 DA. 2. Product page › « Aperçu carte Messenger ».
- Expected: the customer reads the price on the card (« 4 500 DA – 4 800 DA »). The price is the key line of the card (PV 09/11 « the very card the AI sends »).
- Actual: the subtitle is « 40 · Noir · 40 · Blanc · 41 · Noir · 41 · Blanc · 42 · Noir · 42 · Blanc · 4 50… ». The subtitle is built as the variant names, then the price, and is cut at Meta's 80 characters, so the price is the part that gets cut. A customer reads « 4 50… », which may look like 450 DA, or sees no price at all. Six short variant names are enough. The T-shirt with 4 sizes (« S · M · L · XL · 1 500 DA – 1 900 DA ») still fits.
- Likely cause: `apps/api/src/modules/messaging/product-card.ts` `cardElement`. The subtitle is `[photo, c.variants, cardPrice(c)].join(' · ')`, then `cut(…, SUBTITLE_MAX)`. The price should come first, or the variant list should be the part that gets shortened.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J02/shots/fr-s7-card-shoes.png · API `GET /products/01a1e537-beb6-7b43-8512-5aebb3314683/card-preview` → `subtitle: "40 · Noir · … · 42 · Blanc · 4 50…"` (s22.log)
