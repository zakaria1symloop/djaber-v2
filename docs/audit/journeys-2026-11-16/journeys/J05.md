Tested 16/11/2026 17:05–17:50 (Africa/Algiers) on https://djaber.vercel.app with a fresh own shop « Boutique de QA J05 »
(owner `qa-j05-owner@test.djaber.local`, signed up through the real sign-up page). Scripts: `.local/qa/journeys/J05/scripts/`
(one per step, logs `.local/qa/journeys/J05/*.log`), screenshots `.local/qa/journeys/J05/shots/`.

Summary: 17 bugs — Major 3 (J05-1 = ORDERS-01 still live, J05-4, J05-8), Minor 12, Cosmetic 2. Money / stock figures were
identical on every screen at every step (order page, list + KPIs, client list + card, Home, Caisse, Paiements, Journal de
caisse, Ventes par période, Statistiques, products, movements) except the « reste » of closed orders (J05-10).

## Bugs

### J05-1 — Amount fields of the order still turn typed decimals / minus signs into other numbers (ORDERS-01 still live)
- Page: /fr/orders/new (line price, « Montant déjà payé ») and /fr/orders/[id] › « Enregistrer un paiement », inline « QTÉ » of « Modifier » (owner, fr, 1440 px)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. New order, line price: type `2500,00` key by key, Tab. 2. « Montant déjà payé »: type `-100`. 3. On #CMD-1001 › « Enregistrer un paiement » › Montant: type `-100`, then `1000.5`, then `2 000,00`. 4. ARTICLES › Modifier › QTÉ: type `1.5`, then `-3`.
- Expected: « 2 500,00 » read as 2 500 (decimal part dropped), a minus sign refused (the field keeps its value) — as the batch-1 fix promises (packages/ui `NumberInput` comment) and 04 §2 integer DZD.
- Actual (reproduced twice, two different scripts): price `2500,00` → « 250 000 »; paid `-100` → « 10 » (Reste 10 490 DA); payment `-100` → « 10 », `1000.5` → « 10 005 », `2 000,00` → « 200 000 »; QTÉ `1.5` → « 1 », `-3` → « 3 ». No message at all. A pasted / typed « 2 500,00 » still gives a 100× amount. Same cause as J01-1 (caret put back before the separator) — the fix of `packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx` is in the working tree, not on the live site.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J05/s06.log (« price after 2500,00 250 000 », « amountPaid after -100 10 ») · .local/qa/journeys/J05/s13.log (payment dialog lines) · .local/qa/journeys/J05/shots/fr-s13-payment-dialog.png
