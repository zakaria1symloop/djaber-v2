## Bugs

### J08-1 — Fee grid and « Livraison offerte » still turn a minus sign or an exponent into another amount and save it (« -5 » → free delivery from 5 DA) (DELIVERY-01 leftover)
- Page: /fr/delivery/fees (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Grille des frais, row 31 Oran, DOMICILE: type `2500,00`, `1.5`, `-100`, `1e3`, `99999999`, each followed by Tab. 2. « Livraison offerte à partir de »: type `-5`, Enter, then `12500,50`, Enter.
- Expected: integer DZD (04 §2 `validation.price`). A negative or non-integer value is refused with an inline message and never silently turned into another number (DELIVERY-01 « Expected »).
- Actual, before the 18:5x deploy (s05, 18:38): `2500,00` → PUT `homePrice 250000`, `1.5` → 15, `-100` → 100, `1e3` → 13. Threshold `-5` → 5, `12500,50` → 1 250 050. This is the 15/11 bug unchanged.
- Actual, after deploy 28e00c6 (s17, 19:02): decimals are now dropped (`2500,00` → 2 500 and `12500,50` → 12 500 are correct, `1.5` → 1 without a word). Still wrong:
  - `-100` → saved 100 (PUT 200).
  - `1e3` → saved 13.
  - The threshold `-5` → `PATCH /workspace {"freeDeliveryThreshold":5}` → 200. Delivery becomes free on every order of 5 DA or more, with no message.
  - `99999999` is cut to 9 999 999 and then refused with only the generic « Vérifiez les champs. » (API `fields.homePrice: validation.price`).
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J08/s05.log · s17.log · shots fr-s05-row31-invalid.png · API PUT /delivery/fees/31 → 200 `homePrice 100` / `13`; PATCH /workspace → 200 `freeDeliveryThreshold 5`
- Likely cause: packages/ui/src/inputs.tsx `NumberInput` (`allowNegative=false`) drops the « - » and the « e » and keeps the digits instead of refusing the keystroke. FreeDeliveryBar's `Number(draft) <= 0` guard never sees the minus.

### J08-2 — An order with a discount says its delivery fee was « MODIFIÉ » when nobody touched it (« GRILLE SÉTIF · DOMICILE 750 → 750 (MODIFIÉ) »)
- Page: /fr/orders/[id] of #CMD-1003 (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Grille des frais: Sétif domicile 750, « Livraison offerte à partir de » 10 000 DA. 2. Nouvelle commande: Lampe 4 600 + Coussin 2 400 + Porte-clés ×5 (3 000) = sous-total 10 000 DA, remise 500, Sétif / Setif, à domicile, Confirmée. The form quotes « Frais de livraison client : 750 DA · GRILLE SÉTIF · DOMICILE 750 DA » (no override). 3. Enregistrer la commande.
- Expected: the order page repeats the form's explanation, « GRILLE SÉTIF · DOMICILE 750 », and the threshold is read on the same base everywhere. The bar on the fee grid says « … DA de sous-total ». This order's SOUS-TOTAL line shows 10 000 DA, so a merchant reading the bar expects free delivery here. Either the fee is free, or every screen says the rule uses the amount after the discount.
- Actual: the order page (totals line and LIVRAISON › FRAIS CLIENT) and `GET /orders/:id` `feeExplanation` say « GRILLE SÉTIF · DOMICILE 750 → 750 (MODIFIÉ) ». - Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J08/shots/fr-s07-O3-form.png · fr-s07-O3-page.png · .local/qa/journeys/J08/s07.log (« O3 API … "deliveryFee":750 … "feeExplanation":"GRILLE SÉTIF · DOMICILE 750 → 750 (MODIFIÉ)" »)
- Likely cause: apps/api/src/modules/orders/order.service.ts:146 rebuilds the grid quote with `subtotal: o.subtotal` (gross). Create (order.service.ts:266) and the form (order-form.tsx:235) use `subtotal − discount`. When the two differ, line 147 labels the stored fee an override.
