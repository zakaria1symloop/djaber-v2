## Bugs

### J07-1 — A second receipt at a new unit cost re-prices the units already received: the supplier debt grows by 600 DA
- Page: /fr/purchases/[id] › Réceptionner (second, partial → full receipt) (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Nouvel achat #ACH-1001 chez « QA J07 Textile Sétif »: Tapis 5 × 4 000 DA (+ 3 lines, livraison 500, total 35 150) › Commander. 2. Réceptionner: Tapis 3 (coût 4 000), the others in part › Valider. 3. Réceptionner again: Tapis the 2 left, COÛT U. changed to 4 200 (the supplier's new price for this delivery), Pull M 4 for 3 ordered › Valider.
- Expected: the purchase owes what was received at the cost of each receipt: Tapis 3 × 4 000 + 2 × 4 200 = 20 400 DA (04 §4 `purchase.total = Σ(unitCost × qty)`; the weighted-average cost is computed per receipt, 04 §20). The over-receipt of Pull M (+1 250) is the only change of the total.
- Actual: the Tapis line becomes « 5 · 5 · 4 200 DA · 21 000 DA »: the 3 units received at 4 000 are re-priced at 4 200. Total 35 150 → 37 400 (+1 250 over-receipt + 600 re-pricing). Purchase « reste », supplier « solde dû » (29 400), purchases KPI, stock dashboard « DÛ AUX FOURNISSEURS », supplier sheet and the reports « Achats par fournisseur / par période » all carry the extra 600 DA. I paid it in full, so the cash journal shows 37 400 DA paid for 36 800 DA of goods. The report « Achats par produit » says Tapis « coût unit. moyen 4 200 » (real 4 080). The weighted cost of the product is right (1 020, computed per receipt), so the stock value and the debt no longer agree.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J07/shots/fr-s16-receive-rest.png · .local/qa/journeys/J07/s16.log (« total 37 400 · payé 8 000 · reste 29 400 ») · xc-A4.log (product_purchases « Tapis 5 5 21 000 4 200 ») · API POST /purchases/:id/receive → 200, GET /purchases → `total=37400`
- Likely cause: apps/api/src/modules/gds/purchase.service.ts:308-313 — the line keeps one `unitCost` and is rewritten `total: quantity * unitCost` with the cost of the latest receipt.

- Type: Bug
