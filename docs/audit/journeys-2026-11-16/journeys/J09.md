# Journey J09 — Clients & inbox — tested 16/11/2026 (Africa/Algiers)

Tested 16/11/2026 from 19:15 (Africa/Algiers) on https://djaber.vercel.app with a fresh own shop « Boutique de QA J09 »
(owner `qa-j09-owner@test.djaber.local`, signed up through the real sign-up page, onboarding done: stock simple, first
product, preset agent « Vendeur Pro », page connect skipped; stock mode switched to « Avancé » to get counter sales).
Scripts: `.local/qa/journeys/J09/scripts/` (one per step, logs `.local/qa/journeys/J09/*.log`), screenshots
`.local/qa/journeys/J09/shots/`, export files `.local/qa/journeys/J09/data/`.

Summary: 15 bugs — Major 1 (J09-14: the AI sandbox / shared test link take an order from a blocked phone), Minor 9, Cosmetic 5.
Client totals (orders, spent, pills, counts) were identical on list, sheet, full page, menus, dialogs, exports and Home at
every step, except the meaning of « Commandes » (counter sales counted, J09-2) and the order page's FIABLE pill (J09-9).
The inbox could only be tested empty: conversations come only from a connected Meta page (blocked by rule), and the shared
test link / sandbox create none. Retest of 15/11: 19 fixed, 2 still broken (partly), 14 cannot test (+1 out of journey).
