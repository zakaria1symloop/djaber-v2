Live https://djaber.vercel.app, fresh own shop « Boutique de QA J06 » (owner `qa-j06-owner@test.djaber.local`, signed up
through the real sign-up page, onboarding with stock mode « Avancé » so that Ventes comptoir / Caisse exist). Scripts:

## Bugs

### J06-1 — After « Clôturer la journée », counter sales and « Encaisser le reste » keep writing cash rows into the closed day
- Page: /fr/cash (day 16 nov. closed), /fr/sales/new, /fr/sales/[id] › Encaisser le reste (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Major
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Caisse › Clôturer la journée (today): théorique 12 950 DA, compté 12 800, note → closed, row CORRECTION −150, banner « Journée du 16 nov. clôturée … écart −150 DA ». 2. Ventes › Nouvelle vente › Savon 450 DA, Espèces › Valider (#VNT-1011). 3. #VNT-1007 › Encaisser le reste › 300 DA Espèces. 4. Back to Caisse.
- Expected: a closed day takes no more rows (04 §20 « Locked day: any create … of a row dated on a closed day ⇒ 409 CASH_DAY_CLOSED »). The manual row is refused correctly (« Journée du 16 nov. 2026 clôturée. »). A sale or a payment taken after closing is either refused with that message (« rouvrez la journée ») or booked on the next open day. The closed day's figures stay equal to its closing.
- Actual: both are accepted. Rows « Vente #VNT-1011 +450 » (18:13) and « Vente #VNT-1007 (reste encaissé) +300 » (18:14) are dated on the closed 16 nov., under the « clôturée … écart −150 DA » banner (no sign of a later row). `GET /cash/days` → `closing.theoreticalBalance 12 950 / countedBalance 12 800` but `theoreticalCash 13 250`, then 13 550 for the same closed day. Solde actuel moves 27 000 → 27 750. The counted cash and the gap shown no longer describe the day. Reproduced with 2 different paths (new sale, collect).
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J06/shots/fr-s14-cash-closed-later-rows.png · .local/qa/journeys/J06/s13.log (« API days … "theoreticalBalance":12950,"countedBalance":12800 … "theoreticalCash":13250 ») · s14.log
- Likely cause: apps/api/src/modules/gds/cash-rows.ts:13-18 `cashDate()` moves a row of a closed day to `new Date()` (today), and does not check that today is the closed day.
