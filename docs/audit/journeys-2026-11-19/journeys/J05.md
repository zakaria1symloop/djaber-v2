## Bugs

### J05v-1 — Call dialog: after « Sans réponse » the result step offers an enabled « Préparer »; one click puts an order the customer never confirmed into preparation, marked « confirmée »
- Page: /fr/orders/[id] › « Appeler & confirmer » › Sans réponse › step 3 « RÉSULTAT » (owner, fr, 1440) — #CMD-1001, #CMD-1002, #CMD-1005
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. New order #CMD-1005 (NOUVELLE, À APPELER). 2. « Appeler & confirmer » › Continuer › « Sans réponse » › Enregistrer l'appel. 3. On step 3 click « Préparer ».
- Expected: the step says « Sans réponse (1/3) — la commande reste à confirmer. », so the next steps wait for a confirmation: « Préparer » greyed with a reason, like « Envoyer au transporteur » beside it (« Confirmez la commande avant l'envoi. »). 12 §Confirm dialog: « result step (status result, Prepare, Send to courier enabled/disabled with reason, Close) ».
- Actual: « Préparer » is enabled (`disabled=false`, no title). The click sends `POST /orders/:id/status {"to":"preparing"}` → 200. The order becomes EN PRÉPARATION, `confirmationStatus: confirmed`, its stock leaves (`out order_created −1`), and the order is now counted in « Confirmées non envoyées / À expédier ». Nobody reached the customer. The same enabled button was shown after the no-answer call of #CMD-1001 and #CMD-1002 (not clicked there).
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J05v/s11.log (« step3 Préparer 1 disabled false », « API E preparing confirmed ») · s06.log, s08.log (step 3 after « Sans réponse ») · shots fr-s11-E-noanswer-step3.png, fr-s11-E-after-prepare.png
- Note: the status rule allows `new → preparing` (04 §1). The problem is that the dialog offers it right after a failed call, next to a text that says the order is still to confirm.

### J05v-2 — Orders export « Toutes les colonnes »: column « Risque » reads « FIABLE » for every client, including a client with nothing delivered, one whose only order was returned, and one the clients list calls « INCONNU » (J05r-8 leftover)
- Page: /fr/orders › Exporter › Colonnes « Toutes les colonnes (29) » › XLSX (owner, fr)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug
- Steps: 1. Shop with 7 orders: 1 delivered (Amina), 1 returned (Yacine), 1 cancelled (Nadia), 4 open. 2. Export all columns. 3. Compare « Risque » with the clients list.
- Expected: the same word as the screens. The clients list and the orders list only say « FIABLE » for a client with delivered orders (Amina). They show « — » for the others, and « INCONNU » for Nadia (0 orders counted).
- Actual: « Risque » = « FIABLE » on all 7 rows. The raw `normal` of 18/11 is now translated with the label of `enum.Client.riskLevel.normal`, but `normal` only means « not risky ». The merchant reads that a customer who refused the parcel (#CMD-1002, Yacine, 1 return) is reliable.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J05v/shots/export-orders-all-final.xlsx · .local/qa/journeys/J05v/xc-3-final.log (UI clients: only Amina « FIABLE », Nadia « INCONNU »; API clients `risk=normal` for all)
- Likely cause: apps/api/src/modules/orders/order.service.ts `exportTable`: `exportLabel(locale, 'enum.Client.riskLevel', r.riskLevel)` does not use the reliable rule of the screens (`RiskPill`: high → risque, reliable → fiable, else —).
