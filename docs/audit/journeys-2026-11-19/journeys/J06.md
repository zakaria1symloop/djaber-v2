## Bugs

### J06v-1 — After « Modifier le paiement », the Paiements report screen shows the old mode for up to 30 s (the other screens update at once)
- Page: /fr/sales/[id] › Actions › Modifier le paiement, then /fr/reports/payments reached from the sidebar (owner, fr, 1440)
- Severity: Minor
- Type: Bug (figures)
- Steps: 1. Open Rapports › Paiements (row « VNT-1008 … Carte … 1 800 »). 2. Open sale #VNT-1008 › Actions › Modifier le paiement › « Espèces » › Enregistrer. 3. Go back to Rapports › Paiements from the sidebar within 30 s. 4. Wait 35 s, leave the page and come back.
- Expected: the report follows the change like the sale page, the Caisse and the API do (12 §Reports: data are live; same figure on every screen). The merchant fixes the mode just before counting the drawer and checks the report, and sees the right method at once.
- Actual: right after the save, the sale page shows « 1 800 DA Espèces », the Caisse row shows « Vente #VNT-1008 · VENTE · Espèces », and `GET /reports/payments` returns `"method":"cash"`. The report screen still shows « VNT-1008 · Client de passage · Carte · Encaissement · 1 800 ». The same happened on #VNT-1002 (« CCP » shown, API `cash`) and on #VNT-1006 (Carte → CCP: still « Carte » at +3 s, « CCP » only after 35 s). A full reload shows the right mode. The theoretical cash and the KPIs are right. Only the cached rows of the report are stale. Reproduced 3 times in 2 scripts.
- Evidence: .local/qa/journeys/J06v/r1-mode-a.log (« after payments report rows … "method":"cash" » vs « after report UI row … CCP »), r1-mode-b.log (same for VNT-1008), r1-stale.log (« report right after (sidebar nav) … Carte » at 09:44:01, « report after 35 s … CCP »), shots/fr-r1-b-sale-page-after.png
- Likely cause: apps/web/src/features/reports/api.ts:53 — the report query has `staleTime: 30_000`. The sale mutations and `useGdsRefresh` / `useGdsEvents` (apps/web/src/features/gds/api.ts:292-318) invalidate sales, cash, purchases, stock and dashboard, but not `[ws, 'reports']`. The same delay probably applies to every report after any cash write (sale, collect, purchase payment), and `cash.updated` should refresh the open reports.
